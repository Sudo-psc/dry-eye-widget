# Dry Eye Widget App

## Scope

Flutter desktop app for the local floating dry-eye reminder widget.

## Boundaries

- Keep app code under `lib/`, `test/`, `macos/`, and `windows/`.
- Keep the static landing page under `site/` isolated from app feature work.
- Persist user-facing preferences through `WidgetSettings` and `StorageService`.
- Verify meaningful app behavior with targeted tests, full tests, analyzer, or platform build.
