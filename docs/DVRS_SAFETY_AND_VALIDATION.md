# DVRS — safety, validation and data statement

Version: 1.2 editorial contract, 2026-07-13.

DVRS is an educational self-log created by the app author. It has not been validated as a clinical instrument and must not be used for diagnosis, fitness-for-work, treatment selection, employee ranking or organizational decisions. Internal numeric fields are retained for backwards-compatible local history; current primary screens present a descriptive profile instead of a clinical-looking total.

Data are stored locally by default. Export is initiated by the user. The app does not provide employers with an individual dashboard or a corporate decision threshold. Warning answers display safety guidance but do not constitute diagnosis.

Alternatives include keeping a private symptom diary, using non-proprietary environmental checklists, or using a validated/licensed instrument under qualified clinical or research governance. The proprietary log is never required to use pause reminders.

Any future validation claim requires a public protocol, identified target population, comparator, psychometric analysis, independent review, ethics/privacy assessment and versioned results. Until then, use the phrase “educational self-log”, not “risk score” or “validated screening”.
